# Product: Kiro Horror Quiz Show

A kitschy, cute Halloween TV quiz show in the browser, hosted by a ghost MC that talks (Gemini Live voice). 1–3 players use their phones as controllers; the main screen is a pre-made animated stage.

- Tone: a lovable homage to campy late-night horror shows. Cute, warm, analog. **No blood, gore, jump scares or creepy imagery** — in questions, MC lines and UI.
- Show shape: lobby → opening → Act 1 (3 OX, betrayal chance) → Act 2 (4 buzzer, one is a music question) → Act 3 (secret bet + four-choice final) → finale → NEW SHOW.
- Audience for the submission: English speakers. Everything on screen and everything the MC says is English. Players speak English; the MC does not need to understand Korean.
- Never shame players: never say or show "last", "last place", "loser", "worst". The MC teases kindly.
- The stage (visuals, animation, sound) is finished and frozen. This project's work is the engine, server, AI MC, phones, content and integration.
